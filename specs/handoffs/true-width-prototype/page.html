What the proposal changes
  • Bars span exactly their dates. No minimum width and no gutter; the floor is 1 px and corners shrink with the bar.
  • Captions sit right of a bar that is too short. The next item stays on its row if it fits, otherwise drops, and never moves up.
  • Groups and parallels sit above their content. A title over a bracket spanning exactly their dates, then a shaded body padded above and below only.
  • Arrows run on the dates. They leave the middle of the source's last day and land on the middle of the target's first day. Dotted legs are slack. No arrow where position already says it.

Both renders come from the same scheduler, so the dates agree; hover a bar on the right to see its dates. Not drawn yet on the right: isolated include regions, footnotes, labels, owners, links, logos and before: overflow.
