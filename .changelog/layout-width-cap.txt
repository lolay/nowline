```release-note:fixed
**`@nowline/layout` + every surface — `--width` and the other canvas-width options cap the canvas again** ([`specs/rendering.md` § Canvas width](./specs/rendering.md#canvas-width)): `LayoutOptions.width` had no effect since the m2d–m2h content-fit change, so `-w/--width`, `.nowlinerc` `width`, `nowline.preview.width`, `nowline.export.width` and the `width` option of `@nowline/embed`, `@nowline/browser`, `@nowline/export` and the MCP `render` / `export` tools were no-ops. It is now a best-effort maximum: when a roadmap is wider than the cap, its timeline shrinks to fit, down to 1 px per day (`MIN_PIXELS_PER_DAY`, exported with the `cappedPixelsPerDay` helper); captions that spill past narrower bars can still widen the canvas past it. The default is unset (fit the content), which is what every render already did, so output without the option is byte-identical: no example, test fixture, snapshot or determinism cell changes. The documented `(default: 1280)` has not been true since then and is gone from the help, man pages (en, fr), specs and setting descriptions. The MCP `render` tool's `review:true` raster, which re-renders at `width` 1024 to stay small, now actually applies that cap. This also makes the `.nowlinerc` `width` key from the entry above take effect.
```
