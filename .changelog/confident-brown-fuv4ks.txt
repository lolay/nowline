```release-note:changed
A wave with no items is no longer drawn: no hollow marker in the wave strip and no legend entry. When no wave has items, nothing about waves is drawn (no strip, boundaries, hatch or legend), so the roadmap renders as if the wave lines were absent; an empty wave's `after:` floor still schedules. The `NL.I1006` info now names every empty wave in one message, e.g. `Waves "abc", "xyz" and "123" won't be displayed: they have no items assigned.`
```

```release-note:changed
The wave legend shows only the `Background work (not in a wave)` swatch. The `Wave boundary` swatch and the `Waves: #k ...` list are gone; an abbreviated wave label keeps its full title in the cell's SVG tooltip, and footnote marks that don't fit a wave's cell are no longer drawn (the footnote panel still lists them).
```

```release-note:removed
`@nowline/layout` no longer exports `WAVE_EMPTY_MARKER_SIZE_PX`, `WAVE_EMPTY_MARKER_STEP_PX` or `WAVE_LEGEND_ENTRY_GAP_PX`, and `PositionedTimelineScale.waveStrip` loses `placeholder` / `placeholderDraw`, `PositionedWave.strip` loses `marker`, and `WaveLegendEntry.kind` is only `background`. `I1006Args` is now `{ names: string[] }`.
```
