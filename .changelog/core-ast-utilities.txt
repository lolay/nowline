```release-note:fixed
**Breaking: `@nowline/core` + `@nowline/layout` + every surface — an impossible day such as `2026-02-30` is `NL.E0405` on every date key, and the chart reads it as no date** ([`specs/core-ast-utilities.md`](./specs/core-ast-utilities.md) §3.5, §7 decision 5): the validator checked for a real calendar day only on `non-working` and `working` declarations, so `date:2026-02-30` on a milestone or anchor, or `start:2026-02-30` on the roadmap, passed validation and the layout drew it on March 2. `NL.E0405` now fires for it on every `date:`, `start:` and `end:` (in every locale), and the layout (with the schedule the exporters share) and the wave start floor read such a date as no date instead of rolling it into the next month. The layout also reads a year below 100 (`0050-01-01`) as written rather than as 1950. And `every:` on a `non-working` or `working` declaration now rejects a name such as `constructor` or `toString` (`NL.E0904`), which the validator took for a weekday and the layout turned into a rule. No example, test fixture or snapshot changes.
```
