```release-note:added
**Language server: value completions for style keys, `calendar:` and include modes.** Completion now offers each enumerated style key's values (`border`, `shadow`, `font`, `weight`, `italic`, `text-size`, `padding`, `spacing`, `header-height`, `corner-radius`, `bracket`, `header-position`, `timeline-position`, `minor-grid`) in `style` blocks and on `default` lines, the built-in color names after `bg:`, `fg:` and `text:`, the modes after `calendar:` on the roadmap line, and the modes after `config:` and `roadmap:` on an `include` line. The lists are the validator's own tables, now in core's shared value tables.
```
