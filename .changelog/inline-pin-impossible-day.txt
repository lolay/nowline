```release-note:fixed
**Breaking: `@nowline/core` + `@nowline/layout` — an inline date pin on an impossible day such as `after:2026-02-30` is `NL.E0405`, and the chart ignores it** ([`specs/dsl.md`](./specs/dsl.md) Validation Rule 1): making `date:`, `start:` and `end:` strict left `after:` and `before:` pins out, so the validator accepted `after:2026-02-30` and compared the rolled-over March 2 against the roadmap `start:` for `NL.E0413`, while the layout already read it as no date and silently ignored the pin but still drew its calendar glyph. `NL.E0405` now fires for it (in every locale) on an item, group, parallel or a wave's `after:`, pointing at the date element of a list, and `NL.E0412` / `NL.E0413` no longer fire for that pin. The layout draws no glyph for it. No example, test fixture or snapshot changes.
```
