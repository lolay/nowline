```release-note:fixed
**`@nowline/layout` + every surface — a now-line on an axis that grew past the date window now gets its pill row** ([`specs/layout-phases.md`](./specs/layout-phases.md) §1.3 L2, §8 decision 2): the row for the `now` pill was reserved only when today fell inside the roadmap's date window, but the now-line is drawn on the final axis, which grows to the next tick when a bar or marker passes the window's end. With `length:` set, a today between the window's end and the grown axis end drew the pill over the tick labels, with no row for it. The pill row is now reserved whenever the now-line is drawn: the tick panel, wave strip, marker row and chart move down together by the row's 16 px, or by less when a tall beside header card already leaves room above the tick panel. Every file under `examples/` and `tests/` renders as before; `examples/platform-2026.nowline` with `length:1w` and `--now 2026-03-17` is the regression case.
```
