```release-note:removed
**Item `date:` and `start:` pins.** An item can no longer be pinned to a fixed start date: neither the chart nor the exports honour `date:` or `start:` on an item, so the item keeps its place in the lane and starts after the work before it. On an item, a group or a parallel the keys draw the unknown-property warning `NL.W0700` with the hint `Did you mean "after:<date>"?`; use `after:<date>` for "not before this date", and an anchor or a milestone for a named date. The layout warning `NL.W1001` (a wave barrier moved an item's pin) is retired, and the positioned model's `PositionedItem.wavePinOverride` field and the `W1001Args` type are gone. `NL.I1008` now covers only the date in an item's `after:`. Roadmap `start:` and anchor and milestone `date:` are unchanged. See `specs/dsl.md` § "Pinning is not supported".
```

```release-note:fixed
**The chart honours `after:` on a group inside a `parallel`.** A group that is a track of a parallel starts once every item it names in `after:` has finished, as a lane-level group does and as the XLSX, MS Project and Mermaid exports already dated it; before, the chart started it with the parallel. The canvas also grows to a caption spilled from a parallel's track, which was clipped at the chart's right edge.
```

```release-note:fixed
**A `parallel` ends where its last track ends.** The chart no longer adds an 8 px gap after a group track or a parallel before the next item in the lane, so the item after a parallel starts on the date the exports give it (the showcase's Ship now starts on working day 40 in the chart too), and a parallel's box ends at its latest track. The `TRACK_BLOCK_TAIL_GUTTER_PX` export of `@nowline/layout` is removed.
```

```release-note:fixed
**A group's spilled caption no longer delays the next item in the chart.** When a group's last caption runs past its box, the item after the group now starts when the group's work ends, on the date the exports give it, and drops below the caption instead of starting later on the same row. The waves sample's Launch page now starts about 10.8 working days earlier in the chart.
```

```release-note:fixed
**A short bar drawn at its minimum width no longer delays the work after it.** The chart draws a bar too short to see at a minimum width, and that width used to push later than the exports the next item in its lane, the item after a `parallel` the bar ends, any item that names it in `after:`, and the end of its wave. All now follow the item's duration, so those items start on the date the exports give them, and the next item in the lane drops a row when it would overlap the wider bar. An item that names that `parallel` in `after:` now starts in the chart at the parallel's latest track end, not at the wider bar's edge.
```

```release-note:fixed
**An isolated region's lanes honour its own anchors in the chart.** A `roadmap:isolate` include whose lane starts `after:` an anchor the included file declares (as each region of the `nested` example does with its `anchor kickoff`) now starts on that anchor's date in the chart, the date the scheduler that sizes the chart's date window already gave it; before, the chart could not see the region's anchors and started the lane at the roadmap start. The region's anchors are still not drawn, and in a roadmap with waves a region's lanes still see only the wave edges, as the scheduler does.
```

```release-note:changed
**A caption that runs past the chart's end is capped one column past it.** When an item's caption spills past the last date column, the chart now adds one column, always with its tick, grid line and date label (even on an axis that labels only every few columns), and the caption ends inside it: a title too long for that column is cut with `…`, and hovering the bar shows the full title as its tooltip. Before, the caption ran on past the axis with no dates under it and widened the chart to fit. Under an explicit `length:` that ends mid-column, the added column is the full one after that partial column. Captions that end inside the chart are unchanged, and so is the axis under an explicit `length:` that already covers them. The positioned model's `PositionedItem` gains `titleTruncated` (set only on a cut caption; `titleLines` holds the cut lines and `title` the full title).
```
