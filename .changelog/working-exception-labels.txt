```release-note:added
**`@nowline/layout` + `@nowline/renderer` + `@nowline/lsp` — titled working days on the chart** ([`specs/working-calendar.md`](./specs/working-calendar.md) §7.4): a dated `working` declaration with a title is now labelled like a named closure, with the days it turns from non-working to working, `Release crunch · +1d`, in the marker row at its first day in either view. It has no cut line, since a working day removes no time, and that is what tells it from a closure. A weekday make-up day inside a dated closure counts, so the label explains the closure's split into two labelled seams (`Winter break · 4d`, `Make-up day · +1d`, `Winter break · 2d`). An untitled `working` declaration and a titled bounded recurrence are still not labelled, and the editor's hover now notes an undrawn title only on a titled bounded recurrence. The positioned model gains `PositionedTimelineScale.workingExceptions` (`PositionedWorkingException[]`: `x`, `from`, `through`, `titles`, `label`, `labelBox`, `labelSide`) beside `nonWorking`, omitted when empty, and `WorkingCalendar` gains `workingExceptionRuns(from, to)`. Files without a titled dated `working` declaration render byte-for-byte as before; [`examples/working-calendar.nowline`](./examples/working-calendar.nowline) gains its `Launch prep Saturday · +1d` label.
```
