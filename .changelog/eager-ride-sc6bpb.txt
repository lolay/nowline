```release-note:changed
A caption that spills past the end of the chart now gets up to 160 px (about 20 characters), or one column when a column is wider, before it is cut with `…`, and the axis grows by the whole columns it needs. At week scale a short title like `Search v2` or `Deploy` now shows in full instead of `Sea…` or `Depl…`.
```

```release-note:changed
A dated milestone whose predecessor finishes after its date now slips instead of pointing back in time: the diamond on the date turns red, a red dotted line runs along its marker row to a second diamond where it actually lands, and its label and cut line move there. The red arrow from the late predecessor back to the date is gone.
```

```release-note:changed
Every isolated region (`roadmap:isolate`) in a chart now ends at the same right edge, just past the diagram's furthest content (bars, spilled captions, anchors, milestones and the now-line) instead of hugging its own bars. Captions near a region's right side stay inside its dashed border.
```

```release-note:changed
The item after a parallel or group now lines up with the block's first track instead of sitting up beside its header and bracket (`Cool` after `Burst A` / `Burst B`, `Deploy` after a `parallel` block). The block's own header and dashed bracket are unchanged.
```
