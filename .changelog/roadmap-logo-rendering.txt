```release-note:fixed
**`@nowline/layout` + `@nowline/renderer` + every surface: the roadmap `logo:` now renders** ([`specs/rendering.md` § Roadmap Header](./specs/rendering.md#roadmap-header)): `logo:` and `logo-size:` parsed but never drew anything, because layout always left the header logo empty. The logo now draws left of the title inside the header card, vertically centered, in both `header-position:beside` and `above`. `logo-size:` sets its height (`xs` 18, `sm` 24, `md` 36, `lg` 48, `xl` 72 px) and the width follows the logo's own aspect ratio, so a larger or smaller source fills the same slot; a logo wider than 160 px scales down to fit, and a logo taller than the text grows the card (`xl` also grows the 72 px `above` band). SVG logos now scale to that box instead of drawing at their own size, and raster logos draw at the box's shape instead of a square. Each pipeline loads the logo before layout with the new `loadLogo` (`@nowline/renderer`) and passes its intrinsic size as `LayoutOptions.logo`; `roadmapLogoRef` and `scaleLogo` are exported from `@nowline/layout`, and `@nowline/export` gains an `onWarning` callback. A missing, unsupported or unreadable logo prints `warning: logo file not found: ...` (or `unsupported logo format` / `logo could not be parsed`) and the chart renders as if no logo were declared; `--strict` makes it an error (exit 3). Roadmaps without `logo:` render byte-identically.
```
