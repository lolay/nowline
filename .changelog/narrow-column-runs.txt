```release-note:fixed
**`@nowline/layout` — week labels no longer vanish through a bounded recurrence under `hide`** ([`specs/working-calendar.md`](./specs/working-calendar.md) §7.2): `non-working fridays every:fri start:2026-07-01 end:2026-08-31` narrows every week in its window to 32 px at `scale:1w`, and the narrow-column rule dropped each of their labels, so the axis was blank for nine weeks and, with a bounded recurrence never labelled, nothing on the chart said why. Two or more consecutive narrowed weeks now form a run, and inside it a label too wide for its column is kept when it fits the column plus half of each unlabelled neighbour, so the run labels every other week (`Jul 06`, `Jul 20`, …), or its majors under `scale:weeks` thinning. A lone narrowed holiday week still drops its label. At the days scale a hidden week start (a Monday holiday or `every:mon`) moves that week's label to its first working day instead of losing it. Every file under `examples/` and `tests/` renders as before; [`tests/non-working-recurrence.nowline`](./tests/non-working-recurrence.nowline) is the new fixture.
```
